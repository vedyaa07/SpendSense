## Brand & Style

This design system combines modern minimalism with high-end editorial clarity tailored for intelligent personal finance and wealth management. Rooted in restraint, it prioritizes legibility, financial confidence, and structural precision over ornamentation.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Trustworthy & Authoritative:** The interface conveys institutional stability without corporate stiffness. Financial decisions require immediate clarity, zero ambiguity, and quiet authority.
- **Calm & Dispassionate:** Money management frequently induces cognitive strain. The system uses subdued warm tones, disciplined contrast, and structured breathing room to maintain a grounding, orderly presence.
- **Editorial & Discerning:** Drawing cues from broadsheet financial publications and high-end ledger systems, it leverages crisp hierarchy, deliberate typography scales, tabular numeral rhythm, and purposeful micro-copy.

### Visual Style
- **Aesthetic Direction:** Editorial Minimalist with functional tabular density.
- **Surface Treatment:** Matte, tactile planes built on warm stone and paper backgrounds. Strictly avoids saturated glows, heavy skeuomorphism, and decorative blurs.
- **Structural Framing:** Hairline structural borders (`1px`), clean grid boundaries, disciplined vertical rhythms, and purposeful cards with subtle tactile delineations.

## Colors

The palette balances warm organic stone neutrals against an understated, authoritative forest green base. Functional semantic signals are calibrated for immediate financial feedback without causing visual alarms.

### Primary & Brand Accents
- **Primary Accent (`#1B4332`):** Deep Forest Green. Used for primary interactions, high-level ledger headers, active selected states, and brand trust marks.
- **Secondary Accent (`#2D6A4F`):** Muted Forest Green. Used for secondary interactive states, active outline rings, badge accents, and hover elevations.

### Semantic Status Colors
- **Positive / Income (`#10B981` / `#2E7D32`):** Inflow, portfolio yield, balanced budgets, and successful transactions.
- **Negative / Overspending (`#EF4444` / `#C62828`):** Outflow deviations, exceeded limits, credit charges, and system alerts.
- **Warning / Pending (`#D97706`):** Approaching budget thresholds, pending clearings, and account verifications.
- **Informational / Neutral Metric (`#475569`):** Neutral benchmarks, static interest indicators, and categorical tags.

### Neutrals & Background Surfaces
- **App Canvas (`#FBFBFA`):** Warm off-white base surface, replicating archival ledger paper.
- **Elevated Canvas / Secondary Surface (`#F4F4F2`):** Cool light slate stone for grouping containers, sidebars, and input surface backdrops.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure light surface reserved for actionable cards, modals, and isolated content blocks.
- **Text Primary (`#121316`):** Deep charcoal/near-black for primary headlines, critical data, and key numbers.
- **Text Secondary (`#52565E`):** Balanced mid-charcoal for subtitles, contextual labels, and metadata.
- **Text Tertiary / Muted (`#8A909A`):** Captions, timestamps, disabled indicators, and currency affix notations.
- **Border / Hairline Dividers (`#E2E4E0`):** Subtle neutral stone border defining card bounds, cell grids, and rules.

## Typography

The typography centers on **Geist**, leveraging its geometric neutrality and technical execution. The typographic approach focuses on data density, vertical baseline discipline, and financial clarity.

### Financial Numeral Execution
- All numeric representations must enforce tabular figures via CSS (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;` or `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1;`).
- Currency indicators (such as INR `₹`) must be positioned immediately flush before the integer or spaced with a fixed narrow non-breaking space. Decimals should render identically in weight to integers, while trailing decimal zeroes must remain visible for currency accuracy (`₹24,500.00`).

### Editorial Hierarchy Rules
- **Large Balances & Stats (`numeric-hero`):** Designed for hero net worth, monthly spend summaries, and overarching account values.
- **Section Headers (`headline-sm`, `headline-md`):** Understated, using medium weights with negative letter-spacing for refined, editorial punch.
- **Table & Ledger Rows (`numeric-table`, `body-sm`):** Snug line-heights calibrated to sit aligned with border-ruled table layouts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model emphasizes purposeful whitespace and structured column divisions, evoking the balance of editorial financial broadsheets.

### Grid & Breakpoint Systems
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid. Outer section margin: `margin` (`2.5rem`). Column gutters: `gutter` (`1.5rem`). Max-width constrained to `1360px` for optimal data scan-line lengths.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid. Outer margin: `1.5rem`. Column gutters: `1rem`. Secondary analytical modules collapse beneath primary balance summaries.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout. Outer canvas margin: `margin-mobile` (`1rem`). Gutter: `gutter-mobile` (`0.75rem`). Data displays shift from multi-column tables to stacked ledger transaction rows.

### Spacing Principles
- **Micro Rhythms (`space-xs` to `space-sm`):** Used between label-and-value relationships, icons and text pairs, and inline badges.
- **Component Breathing Room (`space-md` to `space-lg`):** Internal padding for cards, financial widgets, and interactive panels.
- **Structural Division (`space-xl`):** Vertical separation between macro sections (e.g., Cashflow Breakdown vs. Transaction Stream).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy, blurry drop shadows and plastic layered surfaces. Depth is established through structural plane layering and hairline stone borders.

### Surface Tiers & Tonal Stacking
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** `#FBFBFA`. The broad foundational layer across the application.
- **Container / Grouping Surface (Level 1):** `#F4F4F2`. Inset blocks, summary ribbons, table header bars, and utility rails.
- **Card & Active Panels (Level 2):** `#FFFFFF`. White cards sit atop Level 0 or Level 1, framed by a crisp `1px` border of `#E2E4E0`.

### Outlines & Border Treatment
- Border thickness is strictly `1px`.
- Dividers between table rows or ledger items utilize `#E2E4E0` with zero blur or drop shadow.
- Focused states employ a double ring: a `2px` crisp border in forest green (`#1B4332`) with a `2px` white offset ring.

### Subtle Elevation Overlays
- Floating dialogs, dropdowns, and date pickers use a precise, tight atmospheric shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.04)`.
- Cards under normal conditions possess no drop shadow; their perimeter is articulated entirely by `#E2E4E0`.

## Shapes

The shape system utilizes soft, controlled geometry (`roundedness: 1`), creating an architectural, purposeful aesthetic that aligns with technical and financial data.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Base Controls & Inputs (`rounded`):** `0.25rem` (4px). Checkboxes, badge tags, table row selector states, text inputs, and standard buttons.
- **Structural Cards & Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px). Analytical cards, monthly summary boxes, dialog boxes, and floating sheets.
- **Large Modals & Overlay Drawers (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (12px). Full transaction detail drawers and modal takeovers.
- **Strictly Square (`rounded-none`):** Matrix grids, ledger rows, tab indicators, and progress bars retain crisp `0px` edges for clean horizontal alignment.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1B4332`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, radius `0.25rem`. Hover state transitions to `#2D6A4F`. Active state dims to `#143326`. Font weight is medium (`label-lg`), with generous horizontal padding (`1.25rem`) and compact vertical padding (`0.625rem`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `transparent`, text `#121316`, border `1px solid #E2E4E0`. Hover state transitions to background `#F4F4F2`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Background `transparent`, text `#52565E`. Hover applies text `#121316` on `#F4F4F2`.
- **Destructive:** Background `transparent`, border `1px solid #EF4444`, text `#C62828`. Hover adopts background `#FEF2F2`.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- White background (`#FFFFFF`) framed with `1px solid #E2E4E0`. Padding defaults to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`).
- No surface shadows. Section headings inside cards sit top-left with category subheadings in `label-sm` uppercase muted stone (`#8A909A`).
- KPI values inside cards use tabular figures in `numeric-hero` or `headline-lg`, with inline metric trend pills.

### Chips & Semantic Badges
- Compact height (`24px`), horizontal padding `0.5rem`, border radius `0.25rem`.
- **Positive (Income / Savings):** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#1B4332`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`.
- **Negative (Expense / Debt):** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `1px solid #FECACA`.
- **Warning (Over Budget):** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
- **Category Badge:** Background `#F4F4F2`, text `#121316`, border `1px solid #E2E4E0`.

### Data Tables & Transaction Ledgers
- Table headers: Background `#F4F4F2`, border bottom `1px solid #E2E4E0`, text in uppercase `label-sm`, letter spacing `0.04em`.
- Table rows: Background `#FFFFFF`, height `48px`, border bottom `1px solid #E2E4E0`. Hover alters background to `#FBFBFA`.
- Numeral alignments: Financial values, rates, and date stamps are strictly right-aligned using tabular figures. Category descriptions and merchant titles are left-aligned.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Input heights: `40px`. Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E4E0`, radius `0.25rem`. Text `body-md` in `#121316`.
- Focus state: `1px solid #1B4332` with an ambient zero-blur offset shadow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #1B4332;`).
- Currency input: Affix icon `₹` embedded as a non-editable, muted (`#8A909A`) prefix inside the input field.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Size: `16px x 16px`. Radius `2px` for checkboxes, circular for radios.
- Unchecked: White background with `1.5px solid #D1D5DB`.
- Checked: Deep forest green `#1B4332` with a white checkmark icon or center dot.

### Specialized Financial Components
- **Budget Depletion Gauge:** Minimal `4px` tall linear horizontal track with a stone background (`#E2E4E0`). The filled progress bar is muted forest green (`#2D6A4F`), turning to amber (`#D97706`) at 85% capacity and crimson (`#EF4444`) when overdrawn.
- **Metric Differential Ribbon:** Inline comparative widget indicating delta against historical periods (e.g., `+12.4% vs last month`), displaying a strict `12px` line arrow icon (`↑` / `↓`).
